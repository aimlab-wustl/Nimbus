NIMBUS

Neuromorphic Ising Machine with Block Updates of Spins

Constraints compiled into moves

A higher-order neuromorphic Ising machine whose moves are programmable multi-spin transitions. It enforces constraints through these transitions instead of energy penalties, so the search visits only feasible states, on CPU, GPU and FPGA.

The software package of the paper Compiling Constraints into Neuromorphic Ising Machines, Faiek Ahsan and Shantanu Chakrabartty, 2026
